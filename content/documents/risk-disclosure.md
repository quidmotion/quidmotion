# Risk Disclosure

**Last updated:** 2026-07-28

Investing in real estate through crypto rails involves significant risk, including possible loss of principal.

## Key risks

- **Market risk** — property values and rental income can decline
- **Liquidity risk** — lock-up periods restrict early exit
- **Operational risk** — platform, custody, and counterparty failures
- **Regulatory risk** — rules for crypto and securities may change
- **Technology risk** — software bugs, outages, or security incidents

Projected returns shown in the app are **simulations for product demo purposes** and must not be treated as promises of performance.
