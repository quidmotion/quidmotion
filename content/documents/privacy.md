# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-07-28

QuidMotion processes account data (email, name, KYC status) to operate the investment platform demo.

## Data we store

- Account profile and authentication hashes (local mode)
- Portfolio, ledger, and transaction records
- KYC submission metadata
- Optional lead magnet emails

## How we use data

- Provide dashboard and admin features
- Enforce authorization and compliance workflows
- Improve product experience

## Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Production deployments may use Supabase (Postgres, Auth, Storage) under their DPA.
